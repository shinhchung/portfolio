Quantum Blazar

A market research workspace that connects scanning, historical analysis, and rule-based paper trading.

The problem

Market research involves signals, news, historical context, and changing portfolio state. Keeping these in separate tools makes it harder to inspect why a trading decision was made.

What I built

A Python and FastAPI application combining Stage 2 stock and crypto scanning, breakout scoring, news-impact monitoring, historical backtesting, and a configurable paper-trading engine.

Engineering approach

  • Bring scanning and research into a shared dashboard so candidates can be examined alongside their context.
  • Use configurable rules and logs to make simulated trading decisions inspectable.
  • Keep historical research and paper trading distinct, so a backtest result is not presented as a live trading outcome.
  • Expose a read-only MCP interface for external research and agent workflows.

What this demonstrates

Market-data integration, backend API development, research tooling, and the design of inspectable automated workflows.

This is a research and simulation project. No investment returns or live-trading performance are claimed here. The source repository is private; this page is a high-level project overview.
